## 🌐 Cross-Platform Odds Aggregators

- [Predictefy](https://predictefy.com/) - Aggregates and normalizes odds/volume across Polymarket, Kalshi, and other venues into one terminal for spotting cross-platform arbitrage. Best for: side-by-side odds comparison instead of single-platform dashboards. Team: bootstrapped, built by "Tafcir" and co-founders (per their own dev.to post); no external funding disclosed.
- [PredictionHero](https://predictionhero.com/) - Aggregates live odds across Polymarket, Kalshi, Limitless, Predict.Fun, and Opinion with trending/new/ending-soon views and a "War Index." Best for: broadest platform coverage when hunting for where markets on the same event disagree. Team: not publicly listed; product is in beta.

<a name="trade-copying--automation"></a>

## 🤖 Trade Copying & Automation

- [PolyCopy](https://polycopy.app/) - Non-custodial Polymarket copy-trading dashboard with an algorithmic "Copy Score" grading traders by category track record, plus optional auto-copy bots. Best for: grading traders by category performance rather than raw follower counts. Team: not publicly listed. (Free tier; Premium $30/mo.)
- [Kreo](https://enter.kreo.app/) - Telegram- and web-based non-custodial copy-trading tool for Polymarket and Kalshi wallets, with sub-3-second execution and insider-flow alerts. Best for: fast, filter-driven wallet copying at a flat 0.5% fee, no subscription. Team: not publicly listed.

<a name="trading-terminals--execution"></a>

## ⚡ Trading Terminals & Execution

- [Kairos](https://kairos.trade/) - The most complete execution stack in prediction markets: one terminal and one API across Kalshi, Polymarket, Predict.fun, and Hyperliquid, stacking matching contracts into a single composite order book with NBBO routing that fills at the best price on any venue. Colocated feeds and sub-second streaming books, execution round trips in tens of milliseconds, plus market-making/arbitrage strategy templates, backtesting against recorded books, and institutional REST + WebSocket APIs with a free sandbox - all at $0.00 platform fees. Best for: systematic and cross-venue traders who want one integration and real execution speed instead of four browser tabs. Built by ex-Cboe quants; raised $2.5M led by a16z crypto, with Geneva Trading.
- [Stand](https://www.stand.trade/) - Trading terminal/aggregator for Kalshi and Polymarket with a "counter-trading" feature that auto-fades historically unprofitable wallets. Best for: fading known losing traders instead of copying winners. Team: co-founded by Edward Ridgely (per Finance Magnates/The Defiant coverage); ~$200M annualized volume reported, no public funding disclosed.
- [Traderline](https://traderline.com/) - Free third-party ladder-trading interface with real-time charts and hotkeys for faster order execution on Polymarket and Betfair. Best for: execution speed over analytics; also covers Betfair, not just prediction markets. Team: built by Mythical Technologies Lda (Portugal), operating trading software since ~2011 (self-reported).
- [Predict Parity](https://predictparity.com/) - Lets you trade across multiple platforms from one interface while tracking wallet-level PnL, win rates, and "smart money" wallets on Polymarket and Kalshi. Best for: combining execution with on-chain wallet tracking rather than analytics alone. Team: not publicly listed (a third-party directory attributes it to "a small New York team," unconfirmed).

## 🧾 Portfolio, Tax & Journaling

- [PredictBoox](https://predictboox.com/) - Syncs positions, trade history, and P&L from Kalshi and Polymarket into one dashboard with trade journaling and tax-ready export. Best for: tax-season record-keeping across multiple platforms. Team: not publicly listed. (Pro $19.99/mo, Genius $39.99/mo.)
- [PMIP](https://pmip.io/) - Portfolio terminal tracking live spreads, positions, and P&L across Kalshi and Polymarket, with resolution-rule analysis and an AI analyst chat. Best for: catching mispricing from wording/deadline/resolution-rule mismatches between venues. Team: not publicly listed; pre-launch as of this writing.

<a name="wallet--trader-analytics"></a>

## 🔍 Wallet & Trader Analytics

- [Polysights](https://www.polysights.xyz/) - Analytics-and-execution platform for Polymarket covering market discovery, insider-wallet detection, and a trading terminal with API/CLI access. Best for: surfacing "sharp"/insider wallets ahead of price moves. Team: founded by Tre Upshaw; raised $1.5M backed by Polymarket (per CNBC, June 2026). Currently in beta.
- [Polymarket Analytics](https://polymarketanalytics.com/) - Searchable database of 1M+ Polymarket traders and markets with trader P&L visualization and custom watchlists, refreshed every 5 minutes. Best for: the sheer scale of its historical trader/market database. Team: not publicly listed.
- [PolyScalping](https://polyscalping.org/) - Free, no-signup Polymarket scanner tracking earnings across LP rewards, maker/taker rebates, referrals, and yield, plus leaderboards and fee calculators. Best for: surfacing reward streams Polymarket's own UI doesn't show in one place. Team: two independent developers ("kober" and "d1namit," per their own X posts); no institutional backing.
- [PredictFolio](https://predictfolio.com/) - Free analytics platform tracking Polymarket/Kalshi trader PnL, win rates, and market volume/pricing, with a trader-discovery leaderboard. Best for: free, multi-platform trader performance tracking. Team: operated by ClearCode B.V. (per site footer); no funding disclosed.
- [PolySmartWallet](https://polysmartwallet.com/) - Real-time leaderboard scoring Polymarket traders by PnL, backtested returns, win rate, and slippage resistance, with per-trader profiles and live PnL charts. Best for: a fast, no-login wallet leaderboard - Polymarket-only, narrower than PredictFolio. Team: not publicly listed; fully anonymous operator.

<a name="alerts--whale-monitoring"></a>

## 🐋 Alerts & Whale Monitoring

- [PolymarketScan](https://polymarketscan.org/) - Free real-time Polymarket analytics tracking whale trades and top traders, with alerts pushed via its @PolyWhaleAlerts feed. Best for: public Twitter-feed whale/price-shock alerts rather than in-app-only notifications. Team: not publicly listed; appears to be an independent/solo project.
- [PolyWatchDog](https://polywatchdog.com/) - Real-time Polymarket monitoring with a live dashboard, wallet-level trader search, custom smart alerts (browser + Telegram), and copy-trade notifications. Best for: the broadest single-tool combo of alerting, trader search, and copy-trade notifications. Team: not publicly listed; domain registered February 2026 - worth independent vetting before connecting a wallet.
- [PolyAlertHub](https://polyalerthub.com/) - Real-time Polymarket monitoring delivering smart-money/whale-flow alerts, fresh-wallet and bot-detection signals, and a paper-trading terminal. Best for: conviction/win-rate-scored whale alerts with copy-trade links. Team: not publicly listed. (Self-reported win-rate/ROI stats are marketing claims, not verified performance.)
- [yesno.pm](https://yesno.pm/) - Telegram/Discord/X bot delivering wallet analytics (Sharpe ratio, drawdown, ROI), insider-trade tracking, and group leaderboards for Polymarket and Kalshi. Best for: in-chat trade-call tracking and group leaderboards rather than a standalone dashboard. Team: not publicly listed.

<a name="research--market-discovery"></a>

## 🧠 Research & Market Discovery

- [Alphascope](https://www.alphascope.app/) - AI research workspace aggregating live odds, news-impact analysis, and probability charts across Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold, and Opinion. Best for: AI-scored news-to-price correlation for spotting mispriced markets. Team: not publicly listed. Portfolio tracking and automated strategies are listed as "coming soon."
- [Poly Prediction](https://polyprediction.app/) - Surfaces trending Polymarket markets with live odds, volume, and AI-generated analysis tied to the news driving each market. Best for: quick news-to-market context on trending events. Team: fully anonymous - not disclosed on the site or in community discussions.
- [Poly Helper](https://polyhelper.io/) - Free Chrome/Brave extension embedding 18+ contextual data panels (crypto prices, sports scores, macro data, whale tracking, news) directly into the Polymarket interface. Best for: in-context research without tab-switching. Team: not publicly listed (a third-party directory attributes it to a small team holding a Polymarket Builders Badge, unconfirmed).
- [METAR.ws](https://metar.ws) — Real-time WebSocket API streaming aviation weather observations (METAR) and forecast model data (GFS, ICON, HRRR, JMA MSM) as structured JSON — the same underlying data source used to resolve weather-related contracts on Polymarket and Kalshi. Best for: verifying the actual resolution data behind weather markets instead of guessing from a forecast app. Team: not publicly listed. (Free sandbox tier; paid tiers from €49/mo.)
